Seasonal Health Alerts & Disease Prevention

Stay ahead of weather-triggered illnesses, vector-borne infections, and seasonal risks with verified prevention protocols, self-care checklists, and emergency guidance.

🍂 Active Season: Post-Monsoon & Autumn Health Advisory (India Region)
HIGH RISK • MONSOON

Dengue Fever

Transmitted by day-biting Aedes mosquitoes breeding in stagnant freshwater containers.

🚨 Key Symptoms: High fever (104°F), retro-orbital eye pain, severe joint & muscle aches, petechial rash, low platelets.
🛡️ Prevention: Clear flowerpot/cooler water weekly, apply DEET mosquito repellents, sleep under bed nets.
⚠️ When to see a Doctor: Persistent vomiting, mucosal gum bleeding, or extreme lethargy.
MODERATE RISK • MONSOON

Malaria

Parasitic Plasmodium infection spread through night-biting Anopheles mosquitoes.

🚨 Key Symptoms: Shaking rigors (chills), high fever cycles, profuse sweating, fatigue, anemia.
🛡️ Prevention: Use insecticide-treated bed nets (ITNs), install window wire meshes, wear long sleeves.
⚠️ When to see a Doctor: Fever with shivering, dark colored urine, or yellowing eyes.
MODERATE RISK • WINTER

Viral Influenza (Flu)

Airborne respiratory virus spreading through droplets and enclosed indoor crowds.

🚨 Key Symptoms: Sudden fever, dry barking cough, sore throat, severe body ache, nasal congestion.
🛡️ Prevention: Annual flu vaccination, mask in crowded spaces, frequent 20-second hand washing.
⚠️ When to see a Doctor: Shortness of breath, chest pressure, or fever lasting > 4 days.
HIGH RISK • MONSOON

Cholera & Waterborne Illness

Bacterial contamination of drinking water and street food after heavy rainfall.

🚨 Key Symptoms: Profuse watery diarrhea ("rice-water stools"), rapid dehydration, nausea.
🛡️ Prevention: Drink only rolling-boiled water, avoid roadside ice/cut fruits, consume warm home meals.
⚠️ When to see a Doctor: Inability to retain fluids, sunken eyes, dry mouth, or tachycardia.
MODERATE RISK • SUMMER

Heat Exhaustion & Heatstroke

Hyperthermia caused by prolonged exposure to high ambient temperatures and humidity.

🚨 Key Symptoms: Dizziness, headache, muscle cramps, intense thirst, confusion, cessation of sweating.
🛡️ Prevention: Drink 3-4 liters water/ORS daily, avoid outdoor exposure between 12-4 PM, wear light cottons.
⚠️ When to see a Doctor: Body temperature > 104°F, confusion, loss of consciousness, or seizures.
MODERATE RISK • MONSOON

Chikungunya

Viral illness characterized by debilitating polyarthralgia and joint swelling.

🚨 Key Symptoms: Severe bilateral joint stiffness, high fever, macular skin rash, morning wrist/ankle pain.
🛡️ Prevention: Strict vector control, eliminate stagnant water in tires/pots, use outdoor mosquito coils.
⚠️ When to see a Doctor: Inability to walk or perform daily activities due to acute joint swelling.
